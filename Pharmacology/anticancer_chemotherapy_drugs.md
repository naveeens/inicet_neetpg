# Anticancer Chemotherapy Drugs

## Overview
Classified by mechanism/cell-cycle specificity. High-yield focus: class-defining toxicities (many drugs are identified purely by their signature side effect in exam vignettes) and cell-cycle phase specificity.

## Cell-Cycle Specificity
- **Cell-cycle non-specific**: Alkylating agents, Antitumor antibiotics (act on both dividing and resting cells) — effective in slow-growing tumors too
- **Cell-cycle specific**: Antimetabolites (S phase), Vinca alkaloids/Taxanes (M phase), Bleomycin (G2) — most effective against rapidly dividing tumors

## Alkylating Agents
| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Key toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cyclophosphamide, Ifosfamide | DNA cross-linking (alkylation) | Lymphoma, breast cancer, autoimmune disease (immunosuppression) | **Hemorrhagic cystitis** (acrolein metabolite — prevented with **mesna** and hydration), myelosuppression, SIADH |
| Cisplatin | DNA cross-linking (platinum-based) | Testicular (near-curative), ovarian, bladder, lung cancer | **Nephrotoxicity** (dose-limiting — prevent with hydration), ototoxicity, peripheral neuropathy, severe nausea/vomiting; minimal myelosuppression compared to other agents |
| Carboplatin | Similar to cisplatin | Similar indications, better tolerated | Myelosuppression (thrombocytopenia) predominant, less nephro/ototoxicity |
| Busulfan | Alkylation | CML, conditioning for bone marrow transplant | Pulmonary fibrosis, hyperpigmentation |
| Nitrosoureas (Carmustine) | Crosses BBB | Brain tumors | Delayed myelosuppression |

## Antimetabolites
| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Key toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Methotrexate | Dihydrofolate reductase inhibitor → ↓ thymidine/purine synthesis (S-phase specific) | Leukemia (ALL), lymphoma, choriocarcinoma, rheumatoid arthritis/psoriasis (low-dose) | Myelosuppression, mucositis, hepatotoxicity, pulmonary fibrosis; **rescued by leucovorin (folinic acid)** |
| 5-Fluorouracil (5-FU) | Thymidylate synthase inhibition (pyrimidine analog) | Colorectal, breast, GI cancers | Myelosuppression, mucositis, **hand-foot syndrome**; toxicity potentiated by leucovorin (used therapeutically to enhance efficacy in colorectal regimens) |
| 6-Mercaptopurine, 6-Thioguanine | Purine analog | ALL maintenance | Myelosuppression, hepatotoxicity; **metabolized by xanthine oxidase — toxicity ↑↑ with allopurinol** (dose reduction needed) |
| Cytarabine (Ara-C) | Pyrimidine analog, DNA polymerase inhibition | AML | Myelosuppression, cerebellar toxicity (high dose), conjunctivitis |
| Hydroxyurea | Ribonucleotide reductase inhibitor | CML, sickle cell disease (↑ HbF), polycythemia vera | Myelosuppression |

*High-yield: Methotrexate toxicity is rescued with leucovorin (bypasses DHFR block by providing reduced folate directly); do not confuse with 5-FU where leucovorin is given to potentiate (not rescue) efficacy.*

## Antitumor Antibiotics
| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Key toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Doxorubicin, Daunorubicin (Anthracyclines)** | Intercalate DNA, inhibit topoisomerase II, generate free radicals | Breast cancer, lymphoma, leukemia | **Dose-dependent cardiotoxicity (dilated cardiomyopathy)** — cumulative dose limiting; dexrazoxane as cardioprotectant; also causes myelosuppression, red urine discoloration (harmless) |
| Bleomycin | Free radical formation, DNA strand breaks (G2 phase) | Hodgkin lymphoma, testicular cancer | **Pulmonary fibrosis** (dose-limiting), minimal myelosuppression — favorable in combination regimens for this reason |
| Dactinomycin | DNA intercalation | Wilms tumor, gestational trophoblastic disease | Myelosuppression |

## Mitotic Inhibitors
| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Key toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vincristine, Vinblastine | Microtubule assembly inhibition (M phase arrest) | Vincristine: leukemia/lymphoma; Vinblastine: Hodgkin lymphoma, testicular cancer | Vincristine → **peripheral neuropathy** (dose-limiting, minimal myelosuppression); Vinblastine → myelosuppression predominant (opposite profile — classic paired contrast) |
| Paclitaxel, Docetaxel (Taxanes) | Stabilize microtubules (prevent disassembly) | Ovarian, breast, lung cancer | Peripheral neuropathy, myelosuppression, hypersensitivity reactions |

## Topoisomerase Inhibitors
| Drug | Mechanism | Use | Toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Etoposide | Topoisomerase II inhibitor | Testicular, small cell lung cancer, lymphoma | Myelosuppression, secondary leukemia risk |
| Irinotecan, Topotecan | Topoisomerase I inhibitor | Colorectal (irinotecan), ovarian (topotecan) | Diarrhea (irinotecan — severe/cholinergic), myelosuppression |

## Targeted Therapy / Biologics (high-yield exam favorites)
| Drug | Target | Use | Notable toxicity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trastuzumab | HER2/neu monoclonal antibody | HER2-positive breast cancer | Cardiotoxicity (reversible, unlike anthracyclines) |
| Imatinib | BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase inhibitor | CML (Philadelphia chromosome), GIST | Fluid retention, hepatotoxicity |
| Rituximab | CD20 monoclonal antibody | B-cell lymphoma, autoimmune conditions | Infusion reactions, hepatitis B reactivation |
| Bevacizumab | Anti-VEGF | Colorectal, renal, ovarian cancer | Hypertension, bleeding, GI perforation risk, impaired wound healing |
| Tamoxifen | Selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM) | ER-positive breast cancer | Endometrial hyperplasia/carcinoma risk (estrogen agonist in endometrium), thromboembolism; hot flashes |
| Cetuximab | EGFR monoclonal antibody | Colorectal, head & neck cancer (KRAS wild-type only) | Acneiform rash |

## Cross-Cutting Toxicity Patterns (exam pattern recognition)
| Toxicity | Drug(s) |
|---|---|
| Cardiotoxicity | Doxorubicin/Daunorubicin (irreversible, dose-dependent); Trastuzumab (reversible) |
| Pulmonary fibrosis | Bleomycin, Busulfan, Methotrexate |
| Nephrotoxicity | Cisplatin |
| Hemorrhagic cystitis | Cyclophosphamide, Ifosfamide (prevent with mesna) |
| Peripheral neuropathy | Vincristine, Cisplatin, Taxanes |
| Minimal myelosuppression (good for combination regimens) | Bleomycin, Vincristine, Cisplatin (relatively) |
| Secondary malignancy risk | Alkylating agents (AML), Etoposide |

## High-Yield One-Liners
- Mesna prevents cyclophosphamide/ifosfamide-induced hemorrhagic cystitis
- Leucovorin rescues methotrexate toxicity but potentiates 5-FU efficacy — opposite roles, same drug
- Doxorubicin cardiotoxicity is cumulative-dose-dependent and largely irreversible; dexrazoxane is cardioprotective
- Bleomycin's dose-limiting toxicity is pulmonary fibrosis, with minimal myelosuppression
- Vincristine → neurotoxicity (minimal myelosuppression); Vinblastine → myelosuppression (minimal neurotoxicity)
- 6-Mercaptopurine toxicity is markedly increased by concurrent allopurinol (both metabolized by xanthine oxidase)
- Cisplatin's dose-limiting toxicity is nephrotoxicity; prevented with aggressive hydration
- Tamoxifen increases endometrial cancer risk while reducing breast cancer recurrence (mixed agonist-antagonist)
- Imatinib targets BCR-ABL fusion protein in Philadelphia-chromosome-positive CML
