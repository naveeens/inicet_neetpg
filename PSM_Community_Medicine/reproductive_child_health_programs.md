# Reproductive Child Health Programs

*Note: RCH/RMNCH+A program components, entitlements, and cutoffs (cash incentives, age limits) are periodically revised by MoHFW. Core program structure/strategy below is stable teaching content — verify exact current amounts/eligibility criteria against the latest scheme guideline.*

## RMNCH+A Strategic Approach
India's continuum-of-care framework covering: **R**eproductive, **M**aternal, **N**ewborn, **C**hild, and **A**dolescent health — emphasizes life-cycle approach with focus on high-priority districts.

## Key Maternal Health Schemes
| Scheme | Purpose | Key Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)** | Promote institutional delivery | Cash assistance to pregnant women (differential amounts for rural/urban, and by state's institutional delivery status) — verify current amounts |
| **Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)** | Eliminate out-of-pocket expenses for delivery | Free delivery (including cesarean), free drugs/diagnostics, free diet, free transport, free treatment of sick newborns up to a defined age |
| **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)** | Fixed-day assured, comprehensive antenatal care | Free ANC check-up on the 9th of every month by a specialist/doctor at designated health facilities |
| **Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)** | Maternity benefit/wage compensation | Cash incentive to pregnant/lactating mothers for first living child (per current scheme rules — verify amount and parity conditions against latest guideline) |

## Key Child Health Programs
| Program | Focus | Key Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram (RBSK)** | Child screening for the "4 D's" | **Defects** at birth, **D**eficiencies, **D**iseases, **D**evelopmental delays/disabilities — screening from birth to 18 years, with referral/management support |
| **Home Based Newborn Care (HBNC)** | Postnatal home visits by ASHA | Structured schedule of home visits in the neonatal period to monitor newborn and support breastfeeding, identify danger signs |
| **Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness (IMNCI)** | Standardized case management approach | Algorithm-based assessment/classification/treatment for common childhood illnesses (pneumonia, diarrhea, malnutrition, etc.) by frontline workers |
| **Facility Based Newborn Care (FBNC)** | Special Newborn Care Units (SNCU), Newborn Stabilization Units (NBSU) | Tiered facility-based care for sick/small newborns |

## Family Planning — Methods Overview
| Category | Methods |
|---|---|
| Spacing methods | Condoms, Oral Contraceptive Pills (OCPs), Intrauterine Contraceptive Device (IUCD/Cu-T), Injectables (e.g., DMPA), Emergency contraception |
| Permanent methods | Female sterilization (tubectomy/laparoscopic sterilization) — most commonly used permanent method in India; Male sterilization (vasectomy/NSV — No-Scalpel Vasectomy) |

- **No-Scalpel Vasectomy (NSV)**: minimally invasive technique, promoted to increase male participation in family planning in India
- Female sterilization remains far more commonly used than male sterilization in India — a persistent gender-skew highlighted in PSM teaching

## Mission Indradhanush / Intensified Mission Indradhanush (IMI)
- Special catch-up immunization drives targeting unimmunized/partially immunized children and pregnant women in low-coverage pockets, supplementing routine UIP
- Verify current phase/status against latest program communication

## POSHAN Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission)
- Umbrella program targeting improvement of nutritional outcomes for children, adolescent girls, pregnant women, and lactating mothers
- Aims to reduce stunting, wasting, underweight prevalence and anemia through convergence of nutrition-related schemes (ICDS, etc.)

## Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)
- India's flagship early childhood development program delivered through **Anganwadi Centres**
- Services (classic "six services"): supplementary nutrition, immunization, health check-up, referral services, nutrition & health education, non-formal pre-school education
- Target beneficiaries: children <6 years, pregnant and lactating mothers, adolescent girls (in some components)

## Key Indicators Tracked Under RCH
- Institutional delivery rate, skilled birth attendance rate
- Full immunization coverage
- ANC coverage (≥4 ANC visits — historic WHO-recommended minimum; newer WHO ANC model recommends 8 contacts — verify which standard is being tested)
- Exclusive breastfeeding rate for first 6 months
- Complementary feeding initiation at 6 months

## High-Yield One-Liners
- RBSK screens for the "4 D's": Defects at birth, Deficiencies, Diseases, Developmental delays
- JSY promotes institutional delivery through cash assistance; JSSK removes out-of-pocket costs for delivery and sick newborn care
- PMSMA provides free, fixed-day (9th of every month) assured antenatal care by a specialist
- ICDS delivers its services through Anganwadi Centres, targeting children under 6 and pregnant/lactating women
- Female sterilization remains the most commonly used contraceptive method in India; male sterilization (NSV) remains underutilized
- IMNCI provides a standardized algorithm for frontline health workers to manage common childhood illnesses
- Exclusive breastfeeding is recommended for the first 6 months of life, per standard WHO/RCH teaching
