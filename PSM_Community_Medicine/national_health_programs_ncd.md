# National Health Programs NCD

*Note: Program names/structures for NCDs are periodically revised and integrated under NHM (National Health Mission). Verify current targets/cutoffs against the latest MoHFW guideline; core screening strategies below are stable exam content.*

## National Programme for Prevention and Control of Cancer, Diabetes, CVD and Stroke (NPCDCS)
- Population-based screening for common NCDs at the primary care level (via Health and Wellness Centres under Ayushman Bharat)
- Targets adults **≥30 years** for opportunistic/population-based screening of hypertension, diabetes, and common cancers (oral, breast, cervical) — verify current age cutoff against latest guideline
- Screening tools: blood pressure measurement, capillary blood glucose, oral visual inspection, clinical breast exam, and VIA (Visual Inspection with Acetic acid)/Pap smear for cervical cancer
- Strategy pyramid: health promotion → early diagnosis → treatment → rehabilitation, delivered through a continuum from sub-centre/HWC to district NCD clinics/cardiac care units

## Hypertension and Diabetes Screening (Population-Based)
| Condition | Screening Tool | Diagnostic Threshold (typical teaching) |
|---|---|---|
| Hypertension | BP measurement | ≥140/90 mmHg (per JNC/standard definitions — verify current national program cutoff) |
| Diabetes Mellitus | Capillary blood glucose (random/fasting) | Fasting ≥126 mg/dL or Random ≥200 mg/dL with symptoms (standard WHO/ADA criteria) — verify program-specific screening cutoff used |

*High-yield: Universal opportunistic screening at ≥30 years is the strategy taught for NPCDCS-type NCD screening in India — verify exact age threshold against the current guideline as this is commonly tested and occasionally revised.*

## National Cancer Control — Common Cancers
- **Cervical cancer**: VIA (low-resource screening) or Pap smear; HPV vaccination as primary prevention (part of evolving national immunization strategy — verify current status of HPV vaccine in Universal Immunization Programme)
- **Breast cancer**: Clinical breast examination (CBE) as primary screening tool at primary care level in the national program (mammography reserved for higher-level/diagnostic confirmation)
- **Oral cancer**: Visual inspection given India's high tobacco/areca nut use burden

## National Mental Health Programme (NMHP)
- District Mental Health Programme (DMHP) as implementation arm — integrates mental health into primary healthcare
- Addresses gap in mental health workforce via task-shifting to primary care providers

## National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment (NPCBVI)
- Historically, cataract has been the leading cause of blindness in India — cataract surgery (IOL implantation) is the major intervention
- School eye screening programs for refractive errors
- Other addressed conditions: glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, corneal blindness (includes eye donation promotion)

## National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly (NPHCE)
- Focused on geriatric-friendly OPDs at district hospitals, rehabilitation, and home-based care for the elderly population

## National Tobacco Control Programme (NTCP)
- Implements **COTPA (Cigarettes and Other Tobacco Products Act), 2003** — prohibits smoking in public places, restricts tobacco advertising, mandates pictorial health warnings on tobacco products
- Tobacco cessation services integrated into NCD clinics

## Ayushman Bharat — Two Pillars (Relevant to NCD Care Continuum)
| Pillar | Component |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Health and Wellness Centres (HWCs)** — upgraded sub-centres/PHCs delivering Comprehensive Primary Health Care including NCD screening, closest point of NCD care to the community |
| 2 | **Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)** — health insurance cover for secondary/tertiary hospitalization for economically vulnerable families; cover amount per family per year — verify current amount against latest scheme guideline (historically ₹5 lakh/family/year, subject to revision) |

## High-Yield One-Liners
- NPCDCS uses population/opportunistic screening for hypertension, diabetes, and common cancers typically from age ≥30 years (verify current cutoff)
- Cataract has classically been the leading cause of blindness in India under NPCBVI's focus
- Clinical breast examination is the primary-care-level screening tool for breast cancer in the national program; mammography is a higher-tier diagnostic tool
- VIA and Pap smear are the screening tools for cervical cancer at the primary/secondary care level
- Health and Wellness Centres under Ayushman Bharat are the frontline delivery points for NCD screening as part of Comprehensive Primary Health Care
- COTPA 2003 is the legal framework for tobacco control in India
