# Tumor Markers Paraneoplastic Syndromes

## Tumor Markers — Use for Screening, Monitoring, Recurrence (NOT primary diagnosis — tissue biopsy remains gold standard)
| Marker | Tumor | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **AFP** (alpha-fetoprotein) | Hepatocellular carcinoma, yolk sac (endodermal sinus) tumor | Also ↑ in neural tube defects (amniotic fluid/maternal serum) |
| **CEA** | Colorectal carcinoma | Non-specific, ↑ in many adenocarcinomas & smokers; used for recurrence monitoring |
| **CA-125** | Ovarian carcinoma (epithelial, esp. serous) | Also ↑ in endometriosis, PID, pregnancy — low specificity |
| **CA 19-9** | Pancreatic carcinoma | Also cholangiocarcinoma |
| **CA 15-3** | Breast carcinoma | Monitoring recurrence |
| **PSA** | Prostate carcinoma | Also ↑ in BPH, prostatitis — organ-specific not cancer-specific |
| **hCG** (beta-hCG) | Choriocarcinoma, gestational trophoblastic disease, germ cell tumors | Molar pregnancy monitoring |
| **PLAP** (placental alkaline phosphatase) | Seminoma | |
| **NSE** (neuron-specific enolase) | Small cell lung carcinoma, neuroblastoma | |
| **S-100** | Melanoma, neural tumors, Langerhans cell histiocytosis | Also schwannoma |
| **Bombesin/gastrin-releasing peptide** | Small cell lung carcinoma, neuroblastoma | |
| **Bence Jones protein** (free light chains in urine) | Multiple myeloma | |
| **Chromogranin/Synaptophysin** | Neuroendocrine tumors (carcinoid, small cell carcinoma, pheochromocytoma) | |
| **Calcitonin** | Medullary thyroid carcinoma | |
| **Thyroglobulin** | Follicular/papillary thyroid carcinoma (post-thyroidectomy monitoring) | |
| **5-HIAA (urine)** | Carcinoid tumor | Serotonin metabolite |
| **VMA/HVA (urine)** | Pheochromocytoma, neuroblastoma | Catecholamine metabolites |

*High-yield: No tumor marker is diagnostic alone — always confirm with histopathology. AFP + hCG panel used in germ cell tumor workup/monitoring.*

## Paraneoplastic Syndromes
Signs/symptoms not directly due to tumor mass, invasion, or metastasis — caused by tumor-secreted substances or immune cross-reactivity.

| Syndrome | Mechanism | Classic Tumor |
|---|---|---|
| **SIADH** (hyponatremia) | Ectopic ADH secretion | **Small cell lung carcinoma** |
| **Cushing syndrome** (ectopic ACTH) | Ectopic ACTH | Small cell lung carcinoma, bronchial carcinoid |
| **Hypercalcemia of malignancy** | PTHrP secretion (humoral hypercalcemia) | **Squamous cell carcinoma of lung**, renal cell carcinoma, breast; also direct bone lysis in myeloma/bone mets |
| Polycythemia | Ectopic EPO | Renal cell carcinoma, HCC, hemangioblastoma |
| **Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome** | Antibodies to presynaptic voltage-gated Ca2+ channels | Small cell lung carcinoma |
| **Myasthenia gravis** | Antibodies to postsynaptic ACh receptors | Thymoma |
| **Trousseau sign (migratory thrombophlebitis)** | Hypercoagulability | Pancreatic adenocarcinoma (also other visceral cancers) |
| **Acanthosis nigricans** | TGF-α or EGF secretion | Gastric adenocarcinoma (and other visceral cancers) |
| **Dermatomyositis** | Immune cross-reactivity | Ovarian, lung, GI malignancies (~in older adults) |
| Cerebellar degeneration (anti-Yo, anti-Hu) | Autoantibodies against Purkinje cells | Small cell lung carcinoma, breast, ovarian carcinoma |
| Nephrotic syndrome (membranous nephropathy) | Immune complex deposition | Solid tumors (lung, colon), especially in adults |
| Hypertrophic osteoarthropathy | Clubbing + periosteal new bone formation | Bronchogenic carcinoma |
| Hypoglycemia | Insulin/IGF-2 secretion | Insulinoma, retroperitoneal sarcoma, hepatocellular carcinoma |
| Carcinoid syndrome | Serotonin secretion (once liver metastasis bypasses hepatic first-pass metabolism) | Carcinoid tumor (midgut) |

*High-yield: Small cell lung carcinoma is the single most "prolific" cause of paraneoplastic syndromes — SIADH, ectopic Cushing, Lambert-Eaton, cerebellar degeneration all associated.*
*High-yield: Squamous cell carcinoma of the lung → hypercalcemia (PTHrP); Small cell lung carcinoma → SIADH/Cushing/Lambert-Eaton — classic exam differentiator between the two lung cancer types.*

## Carcinoid Syndrome — Details
- Occurs when serotonin (5-HT) from carcinoid tumor reaches systemic circulation (bypasses liver — either hepatic mets or extra-intestinal primary e.g. bronchial)
- Features: flushing, diarrhea, bronchospasm/wheezing, right-sided cardiac valve fibrosis (tricuspid regurgitation/pulmonary stenosis — left heart spared as lungs metabolize serotonin)
- Diagnosis: ↑ urinary 5-HIAA

## High-Yield One-Liners
- SIADH and ectopic ACTH (Cushing) — classic paraneoplastic duo of small cell lung carcinoma
- Hypercalcemia of malignancy in lung cancer → think squamous cell carcinoma + PTHrP (humoral), not bone metastasis, if no bone lesions seen
- Lambert-Eaton = presynaptic Ca-channel antibodies (SCLC); Myasthenia gravis = postsynaptic ACh-receptor antibodies (thymoma) — do not confuse the two
- Trousseau sign (migratory thrombophlebitis) classically points to pancreatic carcinoma
- AFP + beta-hCG are the key tumor markers followed in germ cell tumors
- CA-125 is used for ovarian cancer monitoring but is nonspecific (also elevated in endometriosis/PID)
- PSA is prostate-organ-specific, not cancer-specific — elevated in BPH and prostatitis too
- Carcinoid syndrome symptoms only appear once serotonin escapes hepatic first-pass metabolism (liver metastasis or non-GI primary)
- Right-sided valvular lesions in carcinoid syndrome (left heart spared due to pulmonary metabolism of serotonin)
- Bence Jones proteinuria = tumor marker for multiple myeloma (monoclonal free light chains)
