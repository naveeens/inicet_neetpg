# Psychiatric Assessment Scales

## Definition
Standardized clinician-rated or self-rated instruments used to screen, diagnose, or grade the severity of psychiatric conditions and track treatment response. High-yield for exams because the number of items, cutoffs, and "what it measures" are frequently tested.

## Depression
| Scale | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAM-D)** | Clinician-rated | 17/21-item; gold standard in research trials for depression severity |
| **PHQ-9** (Patient Health Questionnaire) | Self-rated | 9 items, mirrors DSM-5 MDD criteria; score ≥10 suggests likely depression; also used to monitor treatment response |
| **Beck Depression Inventory (BDI)** | Self-rated | 21 items; assesses cognitive-affective and somatic symptoms |
| **Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS)** | Self-rated | 10 items; screens for **postpartum depression** specifically |
| **Geriatric Depression Scale (GDS)** | Self-rated | Designed for elderly, avoids somatic items that overlap with normal aging/illness |

## Anxiety
| Scale | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hamilton Anxiety Rating Scale (HAM-A)** | Clinician-rated | 14 items; psychic + somatic anxiety symptoms |
| **GAD-7** | Self-rated | 7 items; screens/grades severity of GAD; score ≥10 suggestive |

## Mania
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Young Mania Rating Scale (YMRS)** | Clinician-rated; 11 items; most widely used to grade manic episode severity |

## Psychosis
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **PANSS (Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale)** | Gold standard; rates positive, negative, and general psychopathology domains — 30 items |
| **BPRS (Brief Psychiatric Rating Scale)** | Broader, 18–24 items; assesses general psychopathology (not schizophrenia-specific) |
| **SAPS/SANS** | Scale for Assessment of Positive/Negative Symptoms — separately grades each domain |

## Cognitive/Dementia Screening
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE)** | 30 points; screens cognitive impairment; cutoff <24 suggestive of dementia (adjust for education); domains: orientation, registration, attention/calculation, recall, language, visuospatial |
| **Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA)** | 30 points; more sensitive than MMSE for **mild cognitive impairment**; cutoff <26 |
| **Clock Drawing Test** | Quick visuospatial/executive screening test |
| **Mini-Cog** | 3-item recall + clock drawing — quick primary care screen |

## Substance Use
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **CAGE questionnaire** | 4 items (Cut down, Annoyed, Guilty, Eye-opener) — alcohol use disorder screening; ≥2 positive suggestive |
| **AUDIT** | WHO 10-item tool; more detailed alcohol risk assessment |
| **CIWA-Ar** (Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Alcohol) | Grades severity of alcohol withdrawal, guides benzodiazepine dosing |

## Global Functioning / Severity
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Global Assessment of Functioning (GAF)** | DSM-IV Axis V; 0–100 scale of overall psychological/social/occupational functioning; DSM-5 replaced this with the WHODAS 2.0 |
| **Clinical Global Impression (CGI)** | Simple 1–7 clinician global severity/improvement rating; widely used in drug trials |
| **WHODAS 2.0** (WHO Disability Assessment Schedule) | DSM-5's preferred disability/functioning measure, replacing GAF |

## Suicide Risk
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS)** | Assesses suicidal ideation and behavior severity; widely used in clinical/research settings |
| **Beck Scale for Suicide Ideation (SSI)** | Self-rated intensity of suicidal thoughts |

## Autism/Child Psychiatry
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **CARS (Childhood Autism Rating Scale)** | Behavioral rating for autism spectrum severity |
| **Conners' Rating Scale** | ADHD screening/assessment |
| **Vineland Social Maturity Scale** | Assesses adaptive functioning/social maturity, used in intellectual disability workup |

## Personality/Other
| Scale | Notes |
|---|---|
| **MMPI (Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory)** | Broad personality/psychopathology assessment |
| **Rorschach Test** | Projective test — inkblot interpretation |
| **TAT (Thematic Apperception Test)** | Projective test — story-telling from ambiguous pictures |

## High-Yield One-Liners
- HAM-D and HAM-A = clinician-rated gold standards for depression and anxiety severity respectively in research
- PHQ-9 score ≥10 and GAD-7 score ≥10 are commonly used screening cutoffs in primary care
- YMRS = standard scale to grade severity of a manic episode
- PANSS = gold standard for schizophrenia symptom severity (positive + negative + general psychopathology)
- MMSE cutoff <24/30 suggests cognitive impairment; MoCA is more sensitive for detecting mild cognitive impairment than MMSE
- CAGE ≥2 = positive screen for alcohol use disorder; CIWA-Ar is used to grade/guide treatment of alcohol withdrawal, not to screen for use disorder
- DSM-5 dropped the multiaxial system — GAF (Axis V) was replaced conceptually by WHODAS 2.0
- EPDS is specific for screening postpartum depression
- Rorschach and TAT are classic projective personality tests; MMPI is an objective/structured personality inventory
