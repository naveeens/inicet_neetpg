# Infertility & Assisted Reproduction

## Definition
- **Infertility**: failure to conceive after **12 months** of regular unprotected intercourse (consider earlier evaluation, e.g., after 6 months, in women ≥35 years)
- **Primary infertility**: no prior conception
- **Secondary infertility**: infertility after a prior pregnancy (regardless of outcome)

## Etiology — Approximate Distribution
| Cause | Approximate Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Male factor** | ~30–40% |
| **Female factor — Ovulatory/endocrine** | ~20–25% |
| **Female factor — Tubal/peritoneal** (PID, endometriosis, adhesions) | ~20–25% |
| **Unexplained** | ~10–15% |
| Cervical/uterine factor | Smaller proportion |
| Combined male + female factors | Common overlap |

## Evaluation
### Male Factor
- **Semen analysis** — first-line test (volume, count, motility, morphology per WHO reference values)
- Hormonal evaluation (FSH, LH, testosterone) if abnormal semen parameters
- Further workup: scrotal ultrasound, genetic testing (karyotype, Y-chromosome microdeletion) for severe oligo/azoospermia

### Female Factor
| Test | Assesses |
|---|---|
| Menstrual history + mid-luteal serum progesterone | Ovulation |
| **Hysterosalpingography (HSG)** | Tubal patency, uterine cavity contour — done in follicular phase, after menses, before ovulation |
| Transvaginal ultrasound | Uterine/ovarian pathology (fibroids, PCOS morphology), antral follicle count |
| **Anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH), Day 2–3 FSH** | Ovarian reserve |
| Laparoscopy with chromopertubation | Gold standard for tubal patency/pelvic pathology assessment (endometriosis, adhesions) — more invasive, used selectively |
| Sonohysterography/hysteroscopy | Intrauterine pathology (polyps, septum, submucosal fibroids) |
| TSH, prolactin | Rule out endocrine causes of anovulation |

## Ovulation Induction
| Drug | Mechanism/Notes |
|---|---|
| **Letrozole** | Aromatase inhibitor; **first-line for ovulation induction in PCOS** per current evidence (superior live birth rates vs clomiphene in landmark trial) |
| **Clomiphene citrate** | Selective estrogen receptor modulator; classic first-line agent, still widely used, now considered second-line to letrozole in PCOS-related anovulation per updated evidence |
| **Gonadotropins (FSH ± LH)** | Used for clomiphene/letrozole-resistant cases or controlled ovarian stimulation for IUI/IVF; risk of multiple pregnancy and **ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS)** |
| **Metformin** | Adjunct in PCOS with insulin resistance, improves ovulation response, not a primary ovulation-induction agent alone |

## Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)
| Technique | Indication |
|---|---|
| **Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)** | Mild male factor, cervical factor, unexplained infertility, donor sperm use — requires at least one patent tube |
| **In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)** | Tubal factor infertility, moderate-severe male factor, endometriosis, failed IUI, unexplained infertility, advanced maternal age |
| **Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)** | Severe male factor infertility (oligo/astheno/teratozoospermia, azoospermia with sperm retrieval) |
| **Gamete/embryo donation, surrogacy** | Premature ovarian insufficiency, absent/nonfunctional uterus, repeated ART failure (per legal framework of country) |

### IVF — Basic Steps
1. Controlled ovarian hyperstimulation (gonadotropins)
2. Oocyte retrieval (transvaginal ultrasound-guided)
3. Fertilization (conventional IVF or ICSI)
4. Embryo culture
5. Embryo transfer (fresh or frozen)
6. Luteal phase support (progesterone)

## Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome (OHSS)
- Complication of ovarian stimulation (gonadotropins, sometimes clomiphene) — exaggerated ovarian response
- Features: ovarian enlargement, ascites, pleural effusion, hemoconcentration, risk of thromboembolism, renal impairment in severe cases
- Higher risk in PCOS, young women, high estradiol levels/large number of follicles
- Management: supportive care, fluid/electrolyte monitoring, thromboprophylaxis in severe cases, cabergoline (dopamine agonist) may reduce severity; avoid pregnancy-triggering hCG in high-risk cycles (freeze-all approach)

## Complications of ART
- Multiple pregnancy (higher order with gonadotropins/multiple embryo transfer)
- OHSS
- Ectopic (including heterotopic) pregnancy
- Procedure-related risks (oocyte retrieval — bleeding, infection)

## High-Yield One-Liners
- Infertility workup is initiated after 12 months of unprotected intercourse without conception (earlier if maternal age ≥35)
- Semen analysis is the first-line investigation for male infertility
- HSG assesses tubal patency and uterine cavity, performed in the early follicular phase after menstruation
- Letrozole has replaced clomiphene as first-line ovulation induction agent in PCOS per current evidence, though clomiphene remains widely used/tested as the classic answer
- ICSI is indicated for severe male factor infertility; IUI requires at least one patent fallopian tube
- OHSS is a complication of ovarian stimulation, most severe in PCOS patients, characterized by ascites, ovarian enlargement, and hemoconcentration
- AMH is a marker of ovarian reserve that does not fluctuate significantly across the menstrual cycle, unlike FSH
