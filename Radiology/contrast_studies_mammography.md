# Contrast Studies & Mammography

## Modalities Overview
- **Barium swallow/meal/enema** — luminal GI tract mucosal detail; contraindicated if perforation suspected (use water-soluble contrast, e.g., gastrografin, instead)
- **Intravenous Urography (IVP)** — largely replaced by CT urography but classic signs remain high-yield
- **Mammography** — screening/diagnostic breast imaging; **BI-RADS** lexicon for standardized reporting
- **HSG (hysterosalpingography)** — tubal patency assessment in infertility work-up

## Esophagus — Barium Swallow Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Bird's beak sign** | Smooth, symmetric tapering of the distal esophagus at the GE junction | **Achalasia cardia** |
| **Corkscrew esophagus (rosary bead esophagus)** | Multiple simultaneous tertiary contractions giving a spiral/corkscrew appearance | **Diffuse esophageal spasm** |
| **Feline esophagus** | Fine transverse folds crossing the esophageal lumen | Gastroesophageal reflux disease |
| **Rat-tail sign** | Smooth, tapered luminal narrowing | Peptic/malignant esophageal stricture |
| **Shouldering** | Abrupt, irregular margins with overhanging edges at a stricture | Malignant esophageal stricture (vs smooth taper of benign stricture) |

## Stomach & Small Bowel
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **String sign (Kantor's sign)** | Thread-like lumen of a fixed, rigid, thickened, non-distensible loop of terminal ileum | **Crohn's disease** (terminal ileal stricture) |
| **Cobblestone appearance** | Nodular mucosal pattern from linear ulcers crisscrossing edematous mucosal islands | Crohn's disease |
| **Moulage sign** | Featureless, dilated small bowel loops uniformly coated by barium, with loss of normal fold pattern | Malabsorption (**celiac disease/sprue**) |
| **Coiled-spring sign** | Barium trapped between the folds of intussuscepted/telescoped bowel | Intussusception; also intramural hematoma |
| **Stack of coins sign** | Regularly spaced, thickened valvulae conniventes resembling a stack of coins | Intramural small bowel hematoma (e.g. anticoagulation, Henoch-Schönlein purpura) |

## Colon — Barium Enema Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Apple-core lesion (napkin-ring lesion)** | Short-segment, annular, constricting lesion with irregular, overhanging margins | **Colorectal carcinoma** |
| **Lead-pipe colon** | Rigid, tubular, featureless colon with loss of haustral markings | Chronic **ulcerative colitis** |
| **Collar-button ulcers** | Ulcers that undermine the mucosa, wider at their base than their neck | Ulcerative colitis |
| **Thumbprinting** | Thick, rounded, thumb-like indentations of the bowel wall from submucosal edema/hemorrhage | Ischemic colitis |
| **Saw-tooth pattern** | Serrated luminal margin from multiple small diverticula and muscular hypertrophy | Diverticulosis |
| **Bird's beak sign (colon)** | Smooth tapering at the point of torsion | Sigmoid/cecal volvulus |

## Urography (IVP) Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Cobra head sign** | Focal dilatation of the distal ureter within the bladder, surrounded by a thin radiolucent halo | **Ureterocele** |
| **Spider-leg deformity** | Elongated, splayed, attenuated calyces stretched around masses/cysts | Polycystic kidney disease, renal masses |
| **Goblet sign** | Smooth, cup-shaped dilatation of the ureter just proximal to an obstructing calculus | Ureteric stone |
| **Drooping lily sign** | Downward-displaced, non-functioning/non-opacified lower pole moiety collecting system | Duplex kidney with an obstructed upper pole moiety (ectopic ureter/ureterocele) |
| **Faceless kidney** | Absent normal papillary impressions on a dilated calyceal system | Central/severe hydronephrosis |

## HSG (Hysterosalpingography)
- **Free spill of contrast into the peritoneal cavity** — confirms tubal patency
- **Beading/nodularity of the tube** — salpingitis isthmica nodosa
- **Honeycomb/filling defects in the uterine cavity** — intrauterine adhesions (Asherman syndrome) or submucosal fibroids/polyps

## Mammography — BI-RADS & Classic Findings
| BI-RADS Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Incomplete — needs additional imaging |
| 1 | Negative |
| 2 | Benign finding |
| 3 | Probably benign (<2% malignancy) — short-interval follow-up |
| 4 | Suspicious (2–95% malignancy) — biopsy |
| 5 | Highly suggestive of malignancy (>95%) — biopsy |
| 6 | Biopsy-proven malignancy |

### Calcification Patterns
| Pattern | Description | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Popcorn calcification** | Large, coarse, "popcorn-like" | **Benign — involuting fibroadenoma** |
| **Eggshell/rim calcification** | Thin curvilinear rim around a lucent center | Benign — fat necrosis, oil cyst |
| **Milk of calcium** | Layering calcification, teacup-shaped on lateral (CC view — amorphous; ML view — curvilinear/"teacup") | Benign — microcysts |
| **Dystrophic calcification** | Coarse, irregular | Benign — post-trauma/surgery, radiation |
| **Rod-like/plasma cell mastitis calcification** | Linear, following ductal course | Benign — duct ectasia |
| **Pleomorphic / fine linear branching (casting) calcification** | Irregular, varying size/shape, or fine branching casts | **Suspicious/malignant** — DCIS |

### Mass Findings
- **Spiculated mass with architectural distortion** — highly suspicious for malignancy
- **Halo sign** — thin radiolucent rim around a well-circumscribed mass — favors benign (fibroadenoma, cyst)
- **Skin thickening + peau d'orange + diffuse asymmetric density** — inflammatory carcinoma
- **Tram-track calcification** — parallel linear calcification of vessel walls — benign vascular (medial arterial) calcification, not to be mistaken for malignant casting calcification

## High-Yield One-Liners
- Bird's beak sign appears in both achalasia (esophagus) and sigmoid/cecal volvulus (colon) — context tells you which
- String sign (Kantor's sign) on barium studies = classic for terminal ileal Crohn's stricture
- Apple-core/napkin-ring lesion on barium enema = colorectal carcinoma until proven otherwise
- Lead-pipe colon = chronic ulcerative colitis; cobblestoning = Crohn's disease — classic barium-enema IBD differentiator
- Cobra head sign = ureterocele; drooping lily sign = duplex kidney with obstructed upper pole moiety
- Popcorn calcification = benign fibroadenoma; pleomorphic/fine linear branching (casting) calcification = suspicious for DCIS/malignancy
- BI-RADS 4 and 5 mandate biopsy; BI-RADS 3 gets short-interval (typically 6-month) follow-up, not biopsy
- Free spill of dye on HSG confirms tubal patency — a key infertility work-up finding
