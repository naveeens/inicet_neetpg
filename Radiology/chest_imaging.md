# Chest Imaging

## Modalities Overview
- **CXR (PA erect)** — first-line; AP portable (ICU, trauma) overestimates heart size and magnifies mediastinum
- **Lateral CXR** — localizes lesions in AP/PA blind spots (retrocardiac, retrosternal)
- **HRCT chest** — 1 mm sections; investigation of choice for **interstitial lung disease (ILD)**, bronchiectasis
- **CT Pulmonary Angiography (CTPA)** — investigation of choice for **pulmonary embolism**
- **Fluoroscopy** — diaphragmatic movement (sniff test for paralysis), swallowing studies

## Silhouette Sign
Loss of a normally visible border (heart, aorta, diaphragm) when an opacity of similar density lies in **anatomic contact** with it — localizes the lesion to a specific lobe/segment.
| Border lost | Structure involved |
|---|---|
| Right heart border | Right middle lobe (medial segment) |
| Left heart border | Lingula / left upper lobe |
| Right hemidiaphragm | Right lower lobe |
| Aortic knob | Left upper lobe (apicoposterior) |
| Descending aorta | Left lower lobe (medial basal) |

*High-yield: Presence of a visible border despite adjacent opacity (hilum overlay sign) argues AGAINST that structure being the source — used to prove a mediastinal mass is not cardiac in origin.*

## Classic Named Signs — Airspace & Cavitary Disease
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Air bronchogram** | Air-filled bronchi outlined within opacity | Alveolar/consolidative process (pneumonia); argues against obstructing endobronchial mass |
| **Air crescent sign** | Crescent of air separating fungus ball from cavity wall | Aspergilloma (healing/resolving cavity, e.g. post-TB) |
| **Monod sign** | Same crescent appearance, mobile mycetoma within cavity | Aspergilloma |
| **CT halo sign** | Ground-glass rim around a nodule | Early **angioinvasive aspergillosis** (hemorrhage); also hemorrhagic mets |
| **Reversed halo (target) sign** | Central ground-glass with denser peripheral ring | **Cryptogenic organizing pneumonia** (also mucormycosis, infarct) |
| **Tree-in-bud sign** | Centrilobular nodules with branching opacities resembling a budding tree | Endobronchial spread of infection — **TB**, bronchiectasis, viral/bacterial bronchiolitis |
| **Signet ring sign** | Dilated bronchus (ring) with adjacent smaller pulmonary artery (dot) on cross-section | **Bronchiectasis** |
| **Finger-in-glove sign** | Tubular branching opacities from mucoid-impacted dilated bronchi | **ABPA** (allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis) |
| **Comet tail sign** | Curving bronchovascular bundle sweeping into a peripheral mass | **Rounded atelectasis** |
| **Crazy-paving pattern** | Ground-glass with superimposed interlobular septal thickening | **Pulmonary alveolar proteinosis** (classic); also ARDS, pneumonia, hemorrhage |
| **Head-cheese sign** | Mixture of ground-glass, mosaic attenuation, and normal lung | **Hypersensitivity pneumonitis** |

## Pneumothorax, Pleura & Mediastinum
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep sulcus sign** | Deep, lucent, elongated costophrenic angle | Pneumothorax on a **supine** film |
| **Meniscus sign** | Curved upper margin of fluid against chest wall (erect film) | Pleural effusion / hydropneumothorax |
| **Continuous diaphragm sign** | Air outlines the entire diaphragm continuously beneath the heart | Pneumomediastinum |
| **Naclerio's V sign** | V-shaped air lucency between parietal pleura and mediastinum/left hemidiaphragm | Pneumomediastinum, esophageal rupture (Boerhaave) |
| **Spinnaker-sail sign** | Elevated thymic lobes resembling a ship's sail, in a neonate | Pneumomediastinum in infants |
| **Golden S sign (S sign of Golden)** | Reverse-S contour of the elevated minor fissure around a central mass | **Right upper lobe collapse from a central obstructing bronchogenic carcinoma** |
| **Luftsichel sign** | Crescent of hyperlucent lung between collapsed left upper lobe and aortic knob | Left upper lobe collapse |

## Pediatric / Congenital Heart Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Scimitar sign** | Curved vessel shadow along right heart border resembling a Turkish sword | **Scimitar syndrome** (partial anomalous pulmonary venous return) |
| **Snowman / figure-of-8 sign** | Large "figure-of-8" cardiac silhouette | Supracardiac **TAPVC** |
| **Egg-on-side sign** | Narrow superior mediastinum + globular heart | **Transposition of great arteries (TGA)** |
| **Boot-shaped heart (coeur en sabot)** | Upturned apex from RV hypertrophy, concave main PA segment | **Tetralogy of Fallot** |
| **Water-bottle/flask sign** | Globular, symmetrically enlarged cardiac silhouette | Pericardial effusion |

## Vascular / Embolism & Skeletal Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Westermark sign** | Focal oligemia distal to an occluded vessel | Pulmonary embolism |
| **Hampton's hump** | Peripheral, pleural-based, wedge-shaped opacity | Pulmonary infarct from PE |
| **Fleischner sign** | Enlarged, prominent central pulmonary artery | PE (or lobar pneumonia) |
| **Rib notching** | Scalloping of the **inferior** rib margins from dilated intercostal collaterals | **Coarctation of aorta** |
| **Kerley B lines** | Short horizontal lines at lung periphery/costophrenic angles | Interstitial (septal) edema, pulmonary edema |
| **Bat-wing / butterfly pattern** | Symmetric perihilar opacities sparing the periphery | Cardiogenic pulmonary edema |
| **Photo-negative shadow of pulmonary edema** | Peripheral opacities, sparing the central/perihilar zone (opposite of edema) | **Chronic eosinophilic pneumonia** |
| **Cervicothoracic sign** | Whether a mediastinal mass border disappears above the clavicles helps localize it anteriorly vs posteriorly | Localizing superior mediastinal masses |

## Primary TB Complex
- **Ghon focus** — primary parenchymal lesion (subpleural, usually mid/lower zone)
- **Ghon complex** — Ghon focus + draining hilar/mediastinal lymphadenitis
- **Ranke complex** — calcified Ghon complex (healed primary TB)
- **Miliary pattern** — 1–3 mm uniform nodules diffusely — hematogenous dissemination

## High-Yield One-Liners
- Air bronchogram = alveolar filling process, essentially excludes an obstructing endobronchial mass at that site
- Golden S sign → think central obstructing bronchogenic carcinoma causing RUL collapse
- Deep sulcus sign is the key pneumothorax sign to look for on a **supine** trauma film (meniscus sign needs an erect film)
- Rib notching (inferior) is pathognomonic of coarctation of aorta with well-developed collaterals
- CT halo sign = early invasive aspergillosis (immunocompromised); reversed halo sign = organizing pneumonia
- Tree-in-bud = endobronchial spread, classically active pulmonary TB
- Boot-shaped heart = TOF; egg-on-side = TGA; snowman/figure-of-8 = supracardiac TAPVC — classic congenital heart triad for MCQs
