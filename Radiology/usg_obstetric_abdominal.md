# USG: Obstetric & Abdominal Imaging

## Modalities Overview
- **Transvaginal USG (TVS)** — early pregnancy, adnexal pathology; higher resolution than transabdominal
- **Obstetric USG windows** — Dating scan (6–9 wk, CRL), NT scan (11–13+6 wk), anomaly scan (18–20 wk, TIFFA), growth scan (28–32 wk+)
- **Doppler** — umbilical artery, MCA, ductus venosus (fetal surveillance in growth restriction)
- **Abdominal USG** — first-line for gallbladder, renal, appendix (children/pregnant), scrotal pathology (no radiation)

## Early Pregnancy Signs
| Sign | Description | Significance |
|---|---|---|
| **Double decidual sac sign** | Two concentric echogenic rings around the gestational sac (decidua capsularis + parietalis) | Confirms **true intrauterine gestational sac**, differentiates from pseudo-gestational sac of ectopic pregnancy |
| **Double bleb sign** | Amniotic sac + yolk sac seen as two adjacent "blebs" with the embryonic disc between them | Normal early embryo (~5–6 weeks) |
| **Yolk sac** | First structure visualized within gestational sac | Confirms intrauterine pregnancy; seen before fetal pole |

- **Discriminatory beta-hCG zone**: gestational sac should be visible on TVS by ~1500–2000 mIU/mL; failure to visualize an IUP above this level raises suspicion for **ectopic pregnancy**

## Multiple Pregnancy — Chorionicity
| Sign | Description | Chorionicity |
|---|---|---|
| **Lambda sign (twin peak sign)** | Triangular projection of chorionic tissue extending between the two membranes at the inter-twin junction | **Dichorionic** twins |
| **T sign** | Membrane meets the placenta at a sharp right angle, no intervening chorionic tissue | **Monochorionic** twins |

*High-yield: Chorionicity determination is best and most reliably done in the first trimester.*

## Fetal Anomaly Markers (TIFFA scan)
| Sign | Description | Association |
|---|---|---|
| **Lemon sign** | Scalloped/concave frontal bones giving a lemon-shaped skull | Spina bifida (Chiari II malformation) |
| **Banana sign** | Flattened, anteriorly curved cerebellum effacing the cisterna magna | Spina bifida (Chiari II malformation) |
| **Strawberry-shaped skull** | Skull tapering anteriorly | **Trisomy 18 (Edwards syndrome)** |
| **Cloverleaf skull (Kleeblattschädel)** | Trilobed skull from craniosynostosis | Thanatophoric dysplasia |
| **Choroid plexus cysts** | Cystic spaces within choroid plexus | Soft marker for **trisomy 18** |
| **Nuchal fold thickening (>6 mm at 15–20 wk)** | Thickened nuchal skin fold | Soft marker for **trisomy 21** |
| **Absent/hypoplastic nasal bone** | Nasal bone not ossified/short | Marker for trisomy 21 |
| **Echogenic bowel** | Bowel as echogenic as bone | Marker for aneuploidy, cystic fibrosis, or swallowed blood |
| **Double bubble sign** | Two fluid-filled dilated structures (stomach + duodenum) in upper abdomen | **Duodenal atresia** — associated with Down syndrome |
| **Sandal gap** | Wide gap between the great and second toe | Trisomy 21 |
| **Rocker-bottom feet** | Convex, rounded sole | Trisomy 18 |

## Molar Pregnancy / Placental Signs
- **Snowstorm appearance / "cluster of grapes" / "honeycomb" pattern** — multiple small anechoic cystic spaces intermixed with echogenic tissue, no fetal parts — **complete hydatidiform mole**
- **Placenta previa** — placenta covering/overlying the internal os — diagnosis best confirmed on **TVS** (safe despite bleeding, more accurate than transabdominal)
- **Vasa previa** — fetal vessels crossing the internal os unsupported by placenta/cord — high risk of fetal exsanguination with membrane rupture

## Abdominal USG — Hepatobiliary Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Sonographic Murphy sign** | Maximal tenderness when the probe compresses directly over the visualized gallbladder | Acute cholecystitis |
| **WES sign (Wall-Echo-Shadow)** | Echogenic gallbladder wall + stone interface + posterior acoustic shadow, gallbladder lumen not distinctly seen | Contracted, stone-packed gallbladder (chronic cholelithiasis) |
| **Comet-tail artifact** | Ring-down/reverberation artifact from cholesterol crystals in intramural sinuses | **Gallbladder adenomyomatosis** |
| **Double-barrel shotgun sign** | Dilated intrahepatic bile duct running parallel to the portal vein branch | Biliary obstruction |
| **Parallel channel sign** | Same as above | Biliary dilatation |

## GI Tract Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Target/doughnut sign** | Concentric alternating hypo-/hyperechoic rings on transverse section | Intussusception; also thickened bowel wall in general |
| **Pseudokidney sign** | Longitudinal section of intussusception resembling a kidney (hypoechoic outer bowel wall + echogenic central mucosa/mesentery) | Intussusception |
| **"Donut" sign of appendicitis** | Non-compressible, blind-ending tubular structure, outer diameter >6 mm, target appearance | Acute appendicitis |
| **Hypertrophic pyloric stenosis criteria** | Pyloric muscle wall thickness ≥3 mm and canal length ≥15–17 mm ("cervix sign" — indented antrum on the hypertrophied pylorus) | Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis |

## Genitourinary Signs
| Sign | Description | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Whirlpool sign** | Swirled, twisted appearance of the spermatic cord or ovarian pedicle vessels on color Doppler | Testicular torsion / ovarian torsion |
| **Keyhole sign** | Dilated posterior urethra communicating with a distended bladder, giving a keyhole shape | **Posterior urethral valves** (fetal/neonatal) |
| **Cortical rim sign** | Thin band of preserved enhancing/echogenic cortex over an otherwise abnormal kidney | Renal infarction |

## High-Yield One-Liners
- Double decidual sac sign confirms a true intrauterine gestational sac; absence with a rising beta-hCG above the discriminatory zone raises suspicion for ectopic pregnancy
- Lambda/twin-peak sign = dichorionic twins; T sign = monochorionic twins — determine chorionicity in the **first trimester**
- Lemon + banana sign together = classic Chiari II/spina bifida cranial signature on TIFFA scan
- Snowstorm/cluster-of-grapes appearance with no fetal parts = complete hydatidiform mole
- Sonographic Murphy sign + gallbladder wall thickening + pericholecystic fluid = acute cholecystitis triad
- WES sign = gallbladder packed with stones, often mistaken for a normal/contracted, non-visualized gallbladder
- Target sign (transverse) + pseudokidney sign (longitudinal) = intussusception on USG
- Whirlpool sign on Doppler is the key sign of testicular/ovarian torsion — a surgical emergency
