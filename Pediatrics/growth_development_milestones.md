# Growth Development Milestones

## Growth Parameters (Normal Values — High Yield)

### Weight
- Birth weight (average, Indian term infant): ~2.5–3.2 kg
- Infants **lose 5–10%** of birth weight in first week (regained by day 10)
- Doubles by **5 months**, triples by **1 year**, quadruples by **2 years**
- Formula for weight (kg) 3–12 months: Age(months)/2 + 3
- Formula 1–6 years: Age(years) × 2 + 8
- Formula 7–12 years: (Age(years) × 7 − 5)/2

### Height/Length
- Birth length (average): ~50 cm
- Increases by **50%** by 1 year (~75 cm)
- Doubles by **4 years** (~100 cm)
- Triples by **13 years**
- After 1 year: growth ~5 cm/year until adolescent growth spurt
- Mid-parental height formula used to predict target adult height

### Head Circumference (HC)
- Birth: ~34–35 cm
- 34 cm at birth → 44 cm at 1 year (fastest growth in first year: +12 cm)
  - 0–3 months: +2 cm/month
  - 3–6 months: +1 cm/month
  - 6–12 months: +0.5 cm/month
- HC > Chest circumference at birth; **chest circumference overtakes HC by ~1 year**
- Anterior fontanelle: closes by **12–18 months**
- Posterior fontanelle: closes by **6–8 weeks**

## Developmental Milestones by Age

*Four domains: Gross motor, Fine motor/adaptive, Language, Social/personal*

| Age | Gross Motor | Fine Motor | Language | Social |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 weeks | Head control, follows objects past midline | — | Social smile | Social smile |
| 3 months | Head steady/no head lag when pulled to sit | Follows object past midline, hands unfisted | Coos, vocalizes | Recognizes mother, reaches for familiar objects |
| 4-5 months | Rolls front to back | Reaches for objects (voluntary grasp) | Laughs aloud, squeals | — |
| 6 months | Sits with support, rolls over completely | Transfers objects hand to hand | Monosyllables (ba, da) | — |
| 8 months | Sits without support | Immature pincer grasp (raking) | Bisyllables (mama, baba — no meaning yet) | Stranger anxiety begins |
| 9 months | Stands holding on, crawls | Mature pincer grasp (thumb-index finger) | — | Waves bye-bye, plays peek-a-boo |
| 10 months | Cruises (walks holding furniture) | Index finger approach, points | — | — |
| 12 months (1 yr) | Walks with one hand held / a few steps alone | Immature grasp of pencil (crude scribble) | 1–2 words with meaning ("mama" meaningfully) | Imitates actions, comes when called |
| 15 months | Walks alone well | Tower of 2 cubes, scribbles | 3-6 words | Indicates wants by pointing |
| 18 months | Runs, walks up stairs holding rail | Tower of 3-4 cubes | 10-20 words, names common objects | Copies parents in tasks, temper tantrums |
| 2 years | Walks up/down stairs (both feet per step), jumps | Tower of 6-7 cubes, circular scribble | 2-word phrases/sentences, uses pronouns | Parallel play, asks for food/toilet |
| 3 years | Rides tricycle, stands on one foot briefly, walks upstairs one foot/step | Tower of 9-10 cubes, copies circle | 3-word sentences, knows full name/age/sex | Group play, dresses/undresses partially, knows gender |
| 4 years | Hops on one foot, walks downstairs one foot/step | Copies cross (+), draws man with 3 parts | Counts to 3-4, asks "why" questions | Cooperative play, tells stories |
| 5 years | Skips, good balance | Copies triangle/square, draws man with 6 parts | Names colors, defines words | Plays competitive games, follows rules |

*High-yield: "Rule of 4s" — square by 4 yrs; "Rule of triangle" — triangle by 5 yrs. Cube tower: 2 cubes at 15 mo, then roughly +3 cubes per 3 months up to age 2.*

## Primitive Reflexes (Disappearance Timing — Classic Exam Topic)

| Reflex | Appears | Disappears | Persistence = Red Flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Moro (startle) | Birth | 3-4 months | Beyond 6 months → cerebral palsy/neurologic damage |
| Rooting | Birth | 3-4 months | — |
| Sucking | Birth | 4 months | — |
| Palmar grasp | Birth | 3-4 months (transitions to voluntary grasp) | Beyond 6 months abnormal |
| Plantar grasp | Birth | 9-10 months (before walking) | Persistence delays walking |
| Asymmetric Tonic Neck Reflex (ATNR/fencing) | Birth (best seen 2 mo) | 6 months | Beyond 6 months → CP |
| Positive support/Placing | Birth | 3-4 months | — |
| Parachute reaction | 8-9 months (protective, never disappears) | Persists lifelong | Absence is abnormal |
| Landau reflex | 3 months | 12-24 months | — |

*High-yield: Persistence of primitive reflexes beyond expected age + absence/delay of protective reflexes (parachute, Landau) = strongest early clinical clue to cerebral palsy.*

## Denver Developmental Screening Test (DDST-II)
- Screens 4 domains: gross motor, fine motor-adaptive, language, personal-social
- Age range: birth to 6 years
- Screening tool only — NOT diagnostic; abnormal result needs further formal developmental/neurological evaluation

## Vision and Hearing Milestones
- Fixes and follows light: birth-1 month
- Fixes and follows face/object past midline: 6 weeks-3 months
- Binocular vision/depth perception: 4 months
- Mature visual acuity (~20/20): 5-6 years
- Startles to loud sound (Moro/blink): birth
- Turns head towards sound: 3-4 months
- Localizes sound well: 6-9 months

## Red Flags for Developmental Delay (Exam Favorites)
- No social smile by **3 months**
- No head control by **4-6 months**
- Not sitting without support by **9 months**
- Not walking by **18 months**
- No single words by **18 months**
- No 2-word phrases by **2 years**
- Loss of previously attained milestones (regression) at any age → always pathological, urgent work-up (metabolic/neurodegenerative disorder)

## High-Yield One-Liners
- Weight doubles by 5 months, triples by 1 year, quadruples by 2 years
- Length doubles by 4 years, triples by 13 years
- Head circumference grows fastest in first 3 months (2 cm/month); anterior fontanelle closes by 12-18 months
- Social smile appears at 6 weeks; stranger anxiety begins around 8-9 months
- Moro reflex persisting beyond 6 months is abnormal and suggests cerebral palsy
- Developmental regression (loss of milestones) is never normal — think neurodegenerative/metabolic disease
- DDST-II is a screening, not diagnostic, tool
