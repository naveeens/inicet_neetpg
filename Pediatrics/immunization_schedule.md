# Immunization Schedule

*Note: The Indian National Immunization Schedule (Universal Immunization Programme, UIP) is periodically revised by the Government of India / IAP. Ages and doses below reflect standard teaching per current knowledge — always verify against the latest official UIP/IAP schedule before an exam, as specific ages/combinations are a common source of updates.*

## Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) — Government of India Schedule (per current knowledge, verify against latest)

| Age | Vaccine |
|---|---|
| Birth | BCG, OPV-0, Hepatitis B-birth dose |
| 6 weeks | OPV-1, Pentavalent-1 (DPT+Hep B+Hib), Rotavirus-1, IPV-1, PCV-1 |
| 10 weeks | OPV-2, Pentavalent-2, Rotavirus-2, PCV-2 |
| 14 weeks | OPV-3, Pentavalent-3, Rotavirus-3, IPV-2, PCV-3 (booster) |
| 9 months | Measles-Rubella (MR)-1, Japanese Encephalitis (JE)-1 (in endemic districts), Vitamin A (1st dose) |
| 16-24 months | DPT booster-1, OPV booster, MR-2, JE-2 (endemic areas), Vitamin A (2nd dose onward, 6-monthly up to 5 yrs) |
| 5-6 years | DPT booster-2 |
| 10 years | TT/Td |
| 16 years | TT/Td |

*High-yield: Pentavalent vaccine in UIP = DPT + Hepatitis B + Hib (5-in-1). IPV was added to UIP schedule alongside OPV as part of the polio endgame strategy — fractional IPV given intradermally at 6 and 14 weeks per UIP guidance (verify latest dosing/route).*

## IAP (Indian Academy of Pediatrics) Schedule — Additional/Optional Vaccines
*IAP schedule is generally more comprehensive than UIP and includes additional vaccines recommended for private practice, subject to periodic revision — verify against the latest IAP immunization schedule.*

- **Hepatitis B**: Birth, 6, 10, 14 weeks (or 0, 1, 6 month schedule)
- **Hib**: part of pentavalent/hexavalent combinations
- **PCV (Pneumococcal conjugate vaccine)**: 6, 10, 14 weeks + booster at 12-15 months
- **Rotavirus**: 6, 10, 14 weeks (oral)
- **IPV**: preferred over OPV in private schedules for full inactivated protection
- **MMR**: 9 months (or 12-15 months) and 15-18 months (2nd dose)
- **Varicella**: 15-18 months, 2nd dose at 4-6 years
- **Hepatitis A**: 1 year onward (2 doses, 6 months apart)
- **Typhoid conjugate vaccine (TCV)**: from 6 months, booster at 2 years
- **HPV**: girls (and boys) from 9-14 years, 2-dose schedule if started before 15 years
- **Influenza**: annual, from 6 months

## Live vs Killed/Inactivated Vaccines (Classic Exam Distinction)

| Live Attenuated | Killed/Inactivated/Subunit |
|---|---|
| BCG | DPT (whole cell/acellular) |
| OPV | IPV |
| MMR/MR | Hepatitis A, Hepatitis B |
| Varicella | Hib, PCV, Typhoid (Vi polysaccharide/TCV) |
| Rotavirus (oral) | Rabies (cell culture) |
| Yellow fever | Influenza (injectable) |
| Typhoid (oral Ty21a) | HPV |

*High-yield: Live vaccines generally contraindicated in pregnancy and immunocompromised states (except in specific situations); two live injectable vaccines given on different days need ≥4 weeks gap; can co-administer live + killed together or killed + killed together on same day at different sites.*

## Contraindications to Vaccination
- **True contraindications**: severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to previous dose or vaccine component, encephalopathy within 7 days of previous DPT dose (for pertussis component)
- **NOT contraindications** (common exam traps): mild illness with low-grade fever, current antibiotic therapy, diarrhea, malnutrition, prematurity (vaccinate at chronological age, not corrected age), family history of adverse events, breastfeeding
- Live vaccines: avoid in pregnancy, severe immunosuppression (HIV with severe immunosuppression, malignancy on chemotherapy, high-dose steroids)
- BCG: contraindicated in symptomatic HIV-infected infants

## Cold Chain and Vaccine Vial Monitor (VVM)
- Vaccines stored at 2-8°C at peripheral level; OPV/measles at -15 to -25°C at higher levels
- VVM indicates cumulative heat exposure — vaccine discarded if VVM reaches discard point regardless of expiry date
- "Shake test" used to detect freeze damage in adsorbed vaccines (DPT, TT, Hep B) — freezing causes irreversible loss of potency

## Vitamin A Prophylaxis Programme (National)
- 1st dose at 9 months with measles vaccine: **1 lakh IU** (100,000 IU)
- Subsequent doses every 6 months up to 5 years: **2 lakh IU** (200,000 IU)
- Given to prevent vitamin A deficiency/xerophthalmia, also reduces measles-associated mortality

## High-Yield One-Liners
- BCG given at birth (or as early as possible) — intradermal, right upper arm classically
- OPV given at birth as "zero dose," then 6, 10, 14 weeks + booster
- Pentavalent vaccine = DPT + Hepatitis B + Hib
- Vitamin A megadose accompanies measles vaccination at 9 months (1 lakh IU), then 2 lakh IU every 6 months to 5 years
- Mild fever/URI is NOT a contraindication to vaccination
- Encephalopathy within 7 days of DPT is a true contraindication to further pertussis-containing doses
- Always verify exact current ages/doses against the latest official Government of India UIP schedule before the exam, as revisions occur periodically
